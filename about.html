About

Mitchel Costa • Founder & Principal Consultant

Mitchel Costa is the Founder and Principal Consultant of Zulu Trust Labs LLC. A U.S. Army combat veteran who served as an infantry First Sergeant, he transitioned into cybersecurity and digital modernization after a distinguished military career.

He works as a network security engineer, primarily focused on digital modernization at a nuclear power plant. He holds a Master of Science in Information Technology with a concentration in Operational Technology Cybersecurity and a Bachelor of Technical Management focused on Network Management. His work centers on helping organizations strengthen cybersecurity, secure operational technology environments, and modernize infrastructure without unnecessary complexity.

Mitchel brings hands-on experience in network architecture, virtualization, OT integration, zero trust models, and defense-in-depth strategies. He also serves as a strategic technology and cybersecurity partner to QI Automation LLC, supporting digital modernization initiatives for operational environments.

Based in Southport, North Carolina, he works with clients across Brunswick County and surrounding areas.
